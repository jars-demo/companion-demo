# Why a companion?

> What a companion is, what it is good for, and when to leave it out.

**Time:** 8 minutes

## Goal

Know what you are building and decide whether your app should have one.

## What a companion is

A companion is a small character that lives in a corner of your app. It looks around, wanders
now and then, and answers a few questions when someone taps it. Think of the little receipt that
walks along the bottom of [Receipts](https://wispr.jishanahmed.in), or the friend in the corner
of this page.

It is **not** a chatbot. It does not guess. It only says what you wrote for it.

## What it is good for

- **Personality.** A tool feels friendlier and more memorable.
- **Quick help.** "What is this?", "Is it free?", "Is my data stored?" answered in one tap.
- **Guidance.** Point new visitors at the one thing they should do first.

## When to leave it out

- Forms people fill in under stress (payments, medical, legal).
- Dense dashboards where every pixel of attention matters.
- When you cannot write honest, short answers yet.

## The three rules of a good companion

1. **Calm, not constant.** It moves now and then, never all the time.
2. **Honest.** Fixed answers only. If it does not know, it says nothing.
3. **Kind to everyone.** Usable with a keyboard and a screen reader, and completely still for
   people who prefer reduced motion.

The kit follows all three for you. The rest of the workshop shows how.

## Check it works

Open this site, wait a few seconds, and watch the companion in the bottom-left corner. Tap it.
Then turn on "reduce motion" in your operating system and reload: it should stay still.
